Common questions

How often should I change my oil?

Follow your owner's manual. Many newer cars on synthetic oil go 5,000 to 10,000 miles between changes, and older cars or conventional oil often call for 3,000 to 5,000. Most manuals also set a time limit, often 6 to 12 months, whichever comes first.

Does short-trip or towing driving change the interval?

Yes. Lots of short trips, towing, dusty roads or extreme heat usually count as severe service, which most manuals say needs a shorter interval.
