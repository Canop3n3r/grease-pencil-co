Common questions

Should I repair or replace my car?

A common rule of thumb: if a repair costs more than the car is worth, or more than about a year of payments on a replacement, replacing usually makes more sense. If the car is otherwise reliable and paid off, one big repair is often still cheaper than a new car payment.

What is the 50 percent rule for car repairs?

Some people replace a car once a single repair costs more than half of what the car is worth. It's a quick gut check, not a hard rule. Look at the total cost of keeping the car over the next year or two, not just this one bill.
