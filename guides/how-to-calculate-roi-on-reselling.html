How to Calculate ROI on Reselling (Return, Margin & Profit Per Hour)

ROI (return on investment) answers one question: for every dollar you paid for an item, how many dollars of profit did you get back? It is the fastest way to compare a $3 thrift find with a $60 estate sale buy and see which one actually worked harder for you.

The ROI formula

First work out your real profit, after everything comes out:

Profit = sale price − platform fees − shipping and supplies − what you paid

Then divide profit by what you paid:

ROI = profit ÷ what you paid × 100

If you paid $10 and made $20 profit, your ROI is 200%. Every dollar you put in came back with two more.

Worked example: a cheap find

Sale price$40.00
Platform fees−$5.40
Shipping and supplies−$7.00
What you paid−$8.00
Profit$19.60
ROI ($19.60 ÷ $8)245%

Worked example: a bigger buy

Sale price$120.00
Platform fees−$16.20
Shipping and supplies−$14.00
What you paid−$60.00
Profit$29.80
ROI ($29.80 ÷ $60)about 50%

The numbers in these examples are made up to show the math, and fees vary by platform and category. Get your real fee with the free eBay fee calculator or the Poshmark and Mercari fee calculator, then run the whole flip through the reseller profit calculator.

ROI vs profit margin

People mix these up. They use the same profit but divide by different things:

  • ROI divides profit by what you paid. It tells you how hard your money worked.
  • Margin divides profit by the sale price. It tells you how much of each sale you keep.
Cheap findBigger buy
Profit$19.60$29.80
ROI245%50%
Margin49%25%

The bigger buy made more dollars, but the cheap find made far more per dollar risked. If your sourcing cash is tight, ROI is the number that tells you where to spend it.

Don't forget profit per hour

A 245% ROI on a $19.60 profit is great if the item took 20 minutes to clean, photograph and ship. It is not great if it took two hours of back-and-forth with buyers. Log a rough time for each item and divide:

Profit per hour = profit ÷ hours spent

In the examples above, if the cheap find took 30 minutes it earned $39.20 an hour, and if the bigger buy took one hour it earned $29.80 an hour. Those are example numbers; yours will tell you which categories are worth your evenings.

What is a good ROI for reselling?

There is no official number. Many flippers aim for at least 100% ROI on cheap thrift items, because small items carry a lot of handling time per dollar, and accept a lower ROI on higher-priced items where the dollar profit is bigger. Whatever target you pick, set it before you buy. The max buy price calculator works backward from your target, and how much to pay for items to resell explains the rule. Pair it with sell-through rate so you are not buying high-ROI items that never sell.
