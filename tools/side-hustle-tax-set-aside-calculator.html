How the math works

Net profit is income minus expenses minus your mileage deduction (miles times the rate). Self-employment tax is about 15.3% of 92.35% of that profit. Income tax is estimated at your bracket (plus any state rate) on profit after subtracting half of the SE tax. The total is spread over the quarters and weeks you have left.
