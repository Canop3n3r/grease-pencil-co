eBay vs Poshmark vs Mercari Fees: Which Pays You More?

The same $30 item can put a different amount in your pocket depending on where it sells. The gap is usually a few dollars, but across a year of flips that adds up. Here is how to compare the three big resale apps on what actually reaches you.

How each platform charges you

  • eBay takes a final value fee on most categories of roughly 13.6% of the total, plus about $0.40 per order. Rates vary by category, so check eBay's fee page for yours.
  • Poshmark has long charged a flat $2.95 on sales under $15 and 20% on sales of $15 or more. Buyers usually pay shipping.
  • Mercari has changed its seller fees several times. Check the current selling fee and payment processing fee in the Mercari app before you compare. The examples below use 10% plus 2.9% and $0.50 as example inputs only.

Fees change, so treat these as starting points and confirm in each app. The free eBay fee calculator and Poshmark and Mercari fee calculator let you type in the current rates.

On small sales, Poshmark's flat fee bites hardest. On bigger sales, its 20% is the biggest single cut, but Poshmark buyers often pay more for clothing and shoes, which can cover the gap.

Fees are not the whole story

  1. Sale price beats fee rate. If an item sells for $5 more on one app, that usually outweighs a few percent in fees. Check sold listings on each before you pick.
  2. Speed matters. Money stuck in an item that sits for 90 days costs you. Track days to sell by platform.
  3. Shipping rules differ. If you pay shipping on one app and not another, add it in, as shown in the real eBay profit formula.
  4. Decide before you buy. Use the payout from your best platform to set your max buy price, or run it in the max buy price calculator.
