How the math works

First the calculator takes the expected sold price and subtracts the platform fee, the shipping label, supplies and your gas. What's left is the money available to cover the item and your profit. The max buy price is the lower of two limits: that amount minus your minimum profit, and that amount divided by one plus your ROI target. For example, with $25 left and a 100% ROI target, you can pay up to $12.50, because $12.50 spent turns into $12.50 profit.

Rules of thumb flippers use

  • Price from sold listings from the last 90 days, not from what's currently listed.
  • Cheap items need a higher ROI to be worth the listing and shipping time. A $3 profit rarely covers your hour.
  • If the max buy price is under the tag price, walk away. There's always another shelf.
