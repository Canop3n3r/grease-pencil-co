Common questions

How do I calculate my car's real MPG?

Fill the tank, reset your trip odometer, drive normally, then fill up again. Divide the miles driven by the gallons it took to refill. Averaging several fill-ups gives the most accurate number.

How much will a road trip cost in gas?

Divide the trip miles by your MPG to get gallons, then multiply by the gas price. Add the return leg if it's a round trip.
