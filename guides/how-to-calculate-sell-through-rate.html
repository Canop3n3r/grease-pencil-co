How to Calculate Sell-Through Rate for Reselling (With Examples)

Sell-through rate tells you how fast things actually sell. Resellers use it two ways: to check whether an item is worth buying before you pay for it, and to see whether your own store is moving inventory or just collecting it.

Formula 1: Market sell-through (before you buy)

Search the item on eBay and count two numbers: how many sold in the last 90 days (turn on the Sold items filter) and how many are active right now.

Sell-through = sold ÷ (sold + active) × 100

Some resellers divide sold by active instead. That gives bigger numbers (it can go over 100%), so pick one method and use it every time so your comparisons mean something. This guide uses sold ÷ (sold + active).

Sold in last 90 days24

Market sell-through40%

The numbers in these examples are made up to show the math. Plug in your own counts, or let the free sell-through rate calculator do it.

Formula 2: Your own store's sell-through (after you list)

Pick a period, like a month. Count how many items you sold in that period and how many you had listed at the start plus what you added.

Sell-through = items sold ÷ items available × 100

Added during October20
Items available90
Sold in October18
Monthly sell-through20%

At 20% a month, an average item sits about five months. That is fine for cheap, light items and painful for bulky ones that take up a shelf.

What is a good sell-through rate?

There is no official cutoff, but a common rule of thumb for market comps is:

  • Above 50%: it sells fast. Buy it if the max buy price works.
  • 20% to 50%: it sells, just slower. Buy if the profit is worth the wait.
  • Below 20%: lots of sellers, few buyers. Pass unless it is very cheap and easy to store.

Sell-through is only half the call. A fast seller with a $3 profit is still a bad buy, so run the item through the max buy price calculator too, and read how much to pay for items to resell for the full rule. To compare buys of different sizes, see how to calculate ROI on reselling.

How to raise your own sell-through

  1. Relist or drop the price on anything older than 60 to 90 days.
  2. Send offers to watchers and likers.
  3. Cross-list slow items on a second platform; compare what each one costs with the eBay vs Poshmark vs Mercari fees guide.
  4. Track days-to-sell for every item so you stop buying the categories that sit. The columns are in tracking reseller inventory in a spreadsheet.
