Common questions

What is Poshmark's fee?

Poshmark has long charged a flat $2.95 on sales under $15 and 20% on sales of $15 or more. Confirm current rates in the Poshmark app.

What does Mercari charge sellers?

Mercari's seller fees have changed several times. Enter the current selling fee and payment processing fee shown in the Mercari app to get an accurate result.
