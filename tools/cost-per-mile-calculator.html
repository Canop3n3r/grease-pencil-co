Common questions

How do I calculate cost per mile for my car?

Add up fuel, maintenance, tires, insurance, depreciation and other costs for a period, then divide by the miles driven in that period.

Is my cost per mile the same as the IRS mileage rate?

No. The IRS standard rate is a flat deduction rate. Your real cost can be higher or lower; knowing it tells you whether a gig or delivery run is actually profitable.
