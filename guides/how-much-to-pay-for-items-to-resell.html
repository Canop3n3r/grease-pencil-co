How Much to Pay for Items to Resell (The Max Buy Price Rule)

The profit on a flip is mostly decided at the register, not when it sells. If you pay too much, no amount of good photos fixes it. The fix is to know your max buy price before you pick the item up.

Step 1: Look at sold prices, not asking prices

On eBay, search the item and turn on the Sold items filter. On Poshmark and Mercari, filter to sold listings too. Ignore the highest sale; use a typical price from the last few weeks. Asking prices only tell you what people hope to get.

Step 2: Subtract what the sale costs you

  • Platform fees (eBay is roughly 13.6% plus $0.40 for most categories; Poshmark takes 20% on sales over $15)
  • Shipping, if you pay for the label
  • Supplies like a poly mailer or box
  • Gas or mileage for the sourcing trip, split across what you bought

What is left is your net payout, the money that actually reaches you.

Step 3: Decide your minimum profit and return

Set two floors and hold to them: a minimum dollar profit (many flippers use $10 to $15 so an item is worth the listing time) and a minimum return on cost (100% means you double your money). Your max buy price is the lower of:

  • Net payout minus your minimum profit
  • Net payout divided by (1 + your minimum return)

If that number is below what the tag says, you walk away. That is the whole rule.

Worked example

Typical eBay sold price$40.00
eBay fees (about 13.6% plus $0.40)− $5.84
Shipping label− $8.00
Mailer and gas share− $1.50
Net payout$24.66
Minus $15 minimum profit$9.66
Divided by 2 (100% minimum return)$12.33
Max buy price (the lower one)$9.66

So a $7.99 tag is a buy and a $12.99 tag is a pass. Skip the mental math in the aisle with the free max buy price calculator, which does these steps on your phone with fee presets for each platform.

Habits that keep you honest

  1. Write your cost on the tag or bag the day you buy it.
  2. Track every item from purchase to sale, so you learn which stores and categories really pay. Our guide on tracking reseller inventory in a spreadsheet shows the columns.
  3. Check your real results against the plan with the reseller profit calculator or the eBay profit-after-fees formula.
