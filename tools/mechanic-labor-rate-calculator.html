Common questions

How do I calculate my shop labor rate?

Add up your monthly overhead (rent, insurance, tools, utilities, software) and the pay you need, divide by the hours you can actually bill, then add your target profit margin.

Why are billable hours lower than hours worked?

Diagnosis, cleanup, parts runs and admin time are rarely billable. Most small shops bill 60-80% of hours on the clock.
