Common questions

When are 2026 quarterly estimated taxes due?

Federal estimated payments for tax year 2026 are generally due April 15, June 15 and September 15, 2026, and January 15, 2027 (moved to the next business day on weekends or holidays).

How do I avoid an underpayment penalty?

Generally, paying at least 100% of last year's total tax (110% if your AGI was over $150,000), or 90% of this year's, through withholding and estimates avoids the penalty. See IRS Form 1040-ES.
