How to Calculate Your Cost Per Mile for Gig Driving (DoorDash, Uber, Instacart)

A $8 delivery offer can be a good deal or a money loser depending on how far you drive. The only way to know is to work out your own cost per mile once, then hold every offer up against it. Here is how, with a worked example.

The two kinds of car costs

  • Running costs go up with every mile: gas, oil changes, tires, brakes and other wear items.
  • Fixed costs you pay whether you drive or not: insurance, registration, a car payment, and the value your car loses each month (depreciation). Spread them over the miles you drive in a month to get a per-mile figure.

Add the two together and you have your real cost per mile.

How to work it out (5 steps)

  1. Gas per mile: price per gallon divided by your MPG. Not sure of your MPG? The free MPG and gas cost calculator works it out from a fill-up.
  2. Maintenance and tires per mile: add up what you spent on oil, tires, brakes and repairs last year and divide by the miles you drove. Your receipts or a maintenance record make this easy.
  3. Fixed costs per month: insurance, registration (yearly cost divided by 12), car payment, and an estimate of monthly depreciation.
  4. Fixed costs per mile: divide that monthly total by the miles you drive in a typical month, personal miles included.
  5. Add them up. Gas plus maintenance plus fixed equals your cost per mile. The free cost per mile calculator does the math for you.

Worked example

A 25 MPG sedan driven 2,500 miles a month. All numbers here are example inputs, not averages, so use your own.

Gas: $3.20 per gallon divided by 25 MPG$0.128/mi
Maintenance and tires (from last year's receipts)$0.100/mi
Fixed: insurance $150 + registration $25 + depreciation $200 = $375 a month, divided by 2,500 miles$0.150/mi
Cost per mile$0.378/mi

Using it to judge offers

Count every mile the offer costs you, including the drive back to a busy zone (dead miles), not just the pickup to drop-off distance.

Offer A: $8.00 for 6 total miles, about 25 minutes. Car cost 6 x $0.378 = $2.27$5.73 net, about $13.75/hr
Offer B: $5.00 for 9 total miles. Car cost 9 x $0.378 = $3.40$1.60 net

A simple rule: decide what you want to clear per mile after car costs, add your cost per mile, and decline anything below it. With a $0.38 cost and a $1.00 target, that is about $1.38 per total mile.

Don't forget taxes

Gig income is self-employment income, and your business miles can lower your tax bill if you log them. The IRS lets you use either the standard mileage rate or your actual car expenses, and either way you need a record of each trip.
