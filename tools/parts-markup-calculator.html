Markup vs margin, quickly

Markup is your profit divided by what the part cost you. Margin is your profit divided by what the customer pays. They are not the same number: a 50% markup is only a 33.3% margin. If you want to keep 40 cents of every parts dollar, you need about a 66.7% markup, not 40%.

Common questions

What is the difference between markup and margin?

Markup is profit divided by your cost. Margin is profit divided by the selling price. A part that costs $50 and sells for $75 has a 50% markup but only a 33.3% margin.

How do I convert a target margin to a markup?

Markup equals margin divided by (1 minus margin). For a 40% margin, 0.40 / 0.60 = 66.7% markup.

Should I charge sales tax on parts?

In many states parts are taxable and labor may or may not be. Rules vary by state, so check with your state revenue department.
