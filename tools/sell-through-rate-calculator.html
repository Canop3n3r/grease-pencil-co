How to read the result

  • Above 50%: it sells fast. Buy it if the price works in the max buy price calculator.
  • 20% to 50%: it sells, just slower. Buy if the profit is worth the wait.
  • Below 20%: lots of sellers, few buyers. Pass unless it's very cheap and easy to store.
