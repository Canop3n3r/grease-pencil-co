What to Put on an Auto Repair Invoice (Checklist for Mobile Mechanics)

A clear invoice gets you paid faster, heads off arguments, and gives you a record if a customer comes back with a question. Here’s what to include.

Shop and customer info

  • Your business name and contact info
  • Invoice or repair-order number and date
  • Customer name, phone and email

Vehicle info

  • Year, make, model and engine
  • VIN and license plate
  • Mileage in (and out)

The work

  • The customer’s concern, in their own words
  • Your diagnosis and the work performed
  • Labor lines: description, hours, rate and amount
  • Parts lines: part number, description, quantity, unit price and amount. Note whether parts are new, used or rebuilt.
  • Sublet work (machine shop, alignment) and shop supplies

Totals and payment

  • Labor subtotal, parts subtotal, supplies, tax and total
  • Deposit received and balance due
  • Payment method and date paid

Authorization and warranty

  • A written estimate the customer approved before the work, with a signature or a note of a phone/text approval and when it was given
  • An approval limit: “call me before anything over $___”
  • Your warranty terms on parts and labor
  • Customer signature on pickup

Check your state’s rules. Many states have specific requirements for auto repair estimates, authorizations and invoices. Look up your state’s consumer-protection or auto-repair rules and adjust your forms to match. This checklist is general information, not legal advice.
