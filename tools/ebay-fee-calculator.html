Common questions

How much does eBay take from a sale?

For most categories eBay charges a final value fee of roughly 13.6% of the total sale (including shipping and tax the buyer pays) plus a per-order fee of about $0.30-$0.40. Rates vary by category and store level, so check eBay's current fee page.

Does eBay charge fees on shipping?

Yes. The final value fee is calculated on the total amount the buyer pays, including shipping and sales tax.
